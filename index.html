Poet

Emir Brown-Murillo

Brown is a Frost Place scholar, recipient of Emory University’s 2017 Artistine Mann Award in Poetry and 2020 Excellence in LGBTQ+ Writing Award, Brown was recognized among Georgia’s Best Emerging Poets in 2017. They have authored three poetry books, with work published in Spires Magazine, Quotidian Bagatelle, A Womb With a Heart, and the University of Central Oklahoma’s The Central Dissent. They created Poetry Processor, a digital writing tool for poets.

The block is hot this year—
perhaps I’ll jump in my riot gear

& patrol until sundown towns fall
back in style. Go for a run in my riot gear,

on horseback through cobblestoned
boroughs & become one with my riot gear.

By dusk, I’m disrobed in my brownstone
with my baton & I hump my riot gear;

there’s no longer a face under my mask.
Under orders to trump in my riot gear.

— My Riot Gear
